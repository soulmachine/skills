# Catalog ranking — 2025–26 hot-AI-startup evidence (2026-08-28)

The SKILL.md catalogs are ordered by these tables. **Score = 2·E + F + M** (/20): **E** 0–5 from the
weighted count of corpus companies whose brand uses the affix (weight 2 for the $1B+ valuation /
Forbes AI 50 / CB Insights AI 100 tier, 1 otherwise); **F** fusability (how cleanly it welds to an
arbitrary seed); **M** meaning. "Old #" is the same-day hand ranking (track record + fusability +
meaning + momentum) that the evidence replaced. Matches were hand-vetted so word coincidences don't
count (Cursor≠-or, Harvey≠-y, Daily≠-ly, Airtable≠ai-).

Corpus: 433 unique companies (334 US-HQ; 223 hot-tier) from Forbes AI 50 2026+2025,
CB Insights AI 100 2026, Y Combinator, a16z Top-100 Gen-AI consumer apps (6th ed.), LinkedIn Top
Startups US 2025 (AI-native), 2025–26 mega-round coverage, and the AI dev-tools peer group. Full
corpus + method: `~/github.com/soulmachine/llm-wiki/raw/agent-notes/ai-startup-naming-affixes-2026-08-28.md`
(compiled page `wiki/ai-startup-name-affixes.md`).

## Not in the catalog (from the same corpus)

- **-ai** (trailing "AI" word) — the era's dominant suffix by far (~60 companies, e.g. OpenAI, xAI,
  Scale AI, EliseAI, Surge AI). Redundant on a `.ai` domain; check collisions before using.
- **-lab** singular (3) · **-0** (Dash0, Mem0, v0) · **-gpt** (trademark-adjacent) · **applied-** (4)
  · vertical tails -health, -robotics, -intelligence.
- Zero hot-corpus uses for: go-, fast-, get-, my-, next-, quick-, smart-, -hub, -stack, -box,
  -mate, -pal, -buddy, -space, -pad.
